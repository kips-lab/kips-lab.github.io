KIPS conducts research spanning a variety of topics at the interface between machine learning and statistical methodology. We are based at the Australian Institute for Machine Learning and at the School of Mathematical Sciences at Adelaide University. Until 2022, KIPS was part of the Computational Statistics and Machine Learning group (OxCSML) at the University of Oxford.

Publications

Our research spans a variety of topics at the interface between machine learning and statistical methodology.

  • Robust and trustworthy machine learning
  • Uncertainty quantification
  • Causal reasoning
  • Explainability
  • Large-scale nonparametric and kernel methods
  • Multiresolution data and data across modalities
  • Physics-informed models
  • Measures of dependence and multivariate interaction
  • Hierarchical and deep generative modelling
